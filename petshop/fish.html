General Information About Aquatic Fish

Keeping fish is one of the most serene and visually spectacular hobbies globally. Celebrated for reducing heart rate and generating immediate mental calm, a luxury custom aquarium is a beautiful center for homes. Setting up healthy systems requires stable chemical filtration, structured water cycling, precise temperature regulators, and species-matched organic pellets.

What Do Fish Eat?

Correct organic nutrition varies strictly by species (carnivorous Bettas vs. omnivorous Goldfish):

Rich Proteins

Frozen or freeze-dried bloodworms, brine shrimp, and daphnia to boost coloration.

Flakes & Pellets

Vitamins-fortified sinking pellets or slow-dissolving flakes to support immune health.

Safe Avoidance

Avoid extreme overfeeding (the major cause of ammonia spikes and quick toxic shock).

Common Clinical Diseases & Solutions

1. Ich (White Spot Disease)

Symptoms: Small white spots resembling table salt on fins, scales, heavy gasping, and rubbing against decorations.
Clinical Solution: Slowly increase temperature to 82°F (28°C) over 48 hours to speed up parasite cycles, and apply vet-approved copper sulfate or specialized anti-ich treatments.

2. Fin Rot (Bacterial Infestation)

Symptoms: Frayed, ragged fin edges, fading colors, and visible red inflammation lines.
Clinical Solution: Execute immediate 50% water change, cycle filters, and administer targeted antibacterial treatments (such as Melafix or Erythromycin).
