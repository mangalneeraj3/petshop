The Beginning

Jaipur's Dedicated Pioneer of Compassion

Established in 1957, Samaria Pet Shop was built on a singular, enduring baseline: every pet is an irreplaceable member of the family, and every household deserves the pure happiness they bring. What started as an intimate, veterinary-first sanctuary has grown into Jaipur's absolute landmark for high-quality pet care.

Over six generations, we have maintained a strict, zero-compromise policy on animal housing conditions, health certification profiles, and veterinary-supervised nutrition charts. We don't just bridge pet purchases—we foster permanent, lifelong matches with premium advice.

Our Mission

To strictly provide healthy, fully dewormed, and microchipped pets to families, backed by systematic after-sale vet chats, professional clinical grooming, and specialized diet support systems that ensure your pet's life begins on the best foot.

Our Vision

To stand as India's golden benchmark for animal welfare and pet retail. We consistently establish standards in hygienic breeder checks, and deliver digital advice frameworks to encourage compassionate, highly educated pet ownership nationwide.
