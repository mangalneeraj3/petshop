About the Breed

General Information

Originating in ancient Siam (Thailand), the Siamese Cat is legendary for its striking color-point coat, sapphire blue eyes, and sleek body. They are incredibly talkative and form intense emotional bonds with their human companions.

Behaviour & Temperament

Siamese cats are affectionate, social, and demanding of attention. They will follow you from room to room 'talking' in their distinct loud voice. They thrive on companionship and suffer if left alone.

Health & Care

Prone to dental issues, respiratory infections, and progressive retinal atrophy. Their short coat is easy to groom with weekly brushing. Dental cleaning is critical.
